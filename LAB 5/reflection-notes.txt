While `setTimeout` is waiting, Node.js does not stop completely. It can continue doing other tasks while the timer is running. After the timer finishes, its callback is handled by the Event Loop. When the call stack is free, the callback runs. So, Node.js can handle other work instead of waiting for the timer to finish.
`Promise.all()` starts all three orders at the same time. It does not wait for one order to finish before starting the next one. It waits until all three orders are completed. So, the total time is usually close to the longest delay among the three orders. This is faster than running the orders one by one using separate `await` statements.
